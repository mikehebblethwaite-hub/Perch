# Helping with Perch

Thank you. There are three ways to help, from easiest to hardest.

## 1. Test it and tell us (5 minutes)

1. Follow "Try it" in the [README](README.md) up to the demo.
2. Run `./coop-perch-report`.
3. Paste what it prints into a [test report](https://github.com/mikehebblethwaite-hub/Perch/issues/new?template=test-report.yml).

A report from a slow or unusual machine is just as useful as one from a fast one.

## 2. Tell us what went wrong

Use [Something went wrong](https://github.com/mikehebblethwaite-hub/Perch/issues/new?template=problem.yml). Paste the error
message exactly as it came. "It didn't work" can't be fixed; the error message usually can.

## 3. Change the code

1. Fork the repository on GitHub, make your change, check it still builds (`make`) and that you tried it.
2. Open a pull request. The form asks three things: what you changed, how you tested it, and the agreement below.
3. **Tick the agreement box.** A check on the pull request stays red until it is ticked.

### The agreement, in plain words

Perch is free under the GNU AGPL-3.0, and companies that cannot share their own code buy a commercial licence. That keeps
Perch free for everyone else. For that to work, every change must be allowed under both. So the box says:

> I wrote this change myself, or I have the right to give it. I agree that Mike J Hebblethwaite may share it under the
> GNU AGPL-3.0 and also under paid commercial licences. I keep my own copyright in it.

You still own what you wrote and can use it however you like. You only give permission for it to be shared both ways.

## Small rules

- One change per pull request, please.
- Never put passwords, keys, addresses or anything personal in code, issues or reports.
- Be kind.
